📖 Daycare Caretaker's Handbook

🎯 Controls: Move with W, A, S, D or Arrow Keys (or virtual joystick on iPad). Use Mouse to look around. Press E, Space, or tap 🐾 ACTION to interact.

🪵 Supply Cabin Restock: Visit the cozy timber log cabin in the backyard (or click 🪵) and press E to replenish all your kibble bowls, fresh water jugs, bath sponges, tennis balls, and feather wands for free!

🤲 Pick Up & Carry: With Hands equipped (key 1), approach any pet and press E or tap to pick them up into your arms! Carry them around, or walk to the bathtub, pet beds, or cat tree and press E to place them in!

🍖 Feeding: Select the Kibble Bowl (key 2) and click a pet or the kitchen feeding bowls to refill and feed hungry pets. You can even feed pets in your arms!

🧼 Bathing: Carry a dirty pet to the Grooming Bathtub or select Bath Sponge (key 4) to give them a warm, bubbly spa wash!

🎾 Yard Fun: Equip the Tennis Ball (key 5) and toss it in the outdoor yard. Watch playful puppies dash and fetch it!

💤 Cozy Naps: Pick up and tuck sleepy pets into the plush donut pet beds to dream sweet dreams.

⭐ 5-Star Reviews: When owners return, happy, fed, and groomed pets grant maximum tips and 5-star ratings!
